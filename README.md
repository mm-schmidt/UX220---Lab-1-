# My Favourite Thing I've Ever Written
## In my last year of high school I took a course called “Media Arts”. As part of our final project, we were tasked with writing a paper and presenting on an important technological advancement and how it influenced human culture and the production of art. I wrote my paper on the invention of the steam locomotive.

## The invention of the steam locomotive allowed for artists to travel and create outside of their, then common, studios, influencing an entire art movement of artist painting “en plein air”; prompted the invention of paint in tubes; and led to some of the most iconic paintings in art history.
